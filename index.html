Questions

What can a site like this do?

The demonstration is deliberately simple on the surface, but shows the journey from customer enquiry to business follow-up.

How quickly can customers contact a trade business through a site like this?

Immediately. A live build can make the main enquiry routes available from the first screen, with mobile actions kept easy to reach.

Can customers upload photos?

Yes. A live build can accept controlled photo uploads so customers can show the problem before a visit is arranged.

Can the website capture different types of work?

Yes. Forms can route or tag different job types so the business receives more useful information.

Can this connect to WhatsApp?

Yes. In a live customer build, WhatsApp can be linked to the business's real number or approved messaging workflow.

Can it connect to a real CRM?

Yes, where the CRM provides an appropriate integration method. ORVIA Web can design the handoff around the customer's existing workflow.

Can ORVIA manage the website after launch?

Yes. Ongoing hosting, edits and managed website support can be included as part of the ORVIA Web service.

Do I have to use this technology?

No. ORVIA Web can build using WordPress, Wix, Shopify, Squarespace and other established platforms where they are the right fit. We usually recommend the approach that gives you the best balance of flexibility, ownership and room to grow.
